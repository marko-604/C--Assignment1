\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Strategy for selecting the farthest critter from the tower. 

Implements the \doxylink{class_target_selection_strategy}{Target\+Selection\+Strategy} interface to choose the critter that is farthest along the path (or furthest in terms of distance).
